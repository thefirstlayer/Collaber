A mod that lets you and your friends build a level together, for free. To use, create a empty level. Next, press ESC or go to the pause menu. In the bottom right corner, press the "Collab" button. Make your own unique code and type it in the text box. Share this exact code to everybody who wants to collab. Press join and it works. Make sure that no more than 1 person edits a block at the same time.
